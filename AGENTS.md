2. Do not reintroduce Railway, Docker, Postgres, Redis, BullMQ, or a separate AI worker.

8. Do not move base video rendering or TTS to paid cloud video services.
9. Keep the current UI, CSS, layout and visible control positions unchanged unless explicitly requested.
10. Keep language order Bahasa Indonesia → English → 中文 and default Bahasa Indonesia.
